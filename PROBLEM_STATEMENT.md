# Problem Statement

## Assigned Challenge

Compare crop yields across years and identify crops whose yields are more variable over time.

## Operational Questions

- Which crops show lower or higher relative historical yield variability?
- Which crops have rising or falling yield trends, independently of their variability?
- Does variability appear to change between earlier and later observations?
- What large negative deviations from a fitted long-run trend are present?
- How do recent state and seasonal observations compare where the source provides usable coverage?
- How well do lag-based forecasts perform against a simple previous-year baseline?

## Measurement and Scope

Yield is the primary outcome, measured in Kg/Ha where the source specifies it. Area and production may provide context but are not substituted for yield. National annual analysis uses explicit `Total` observations; Kharif, Rabi, and Summer records are not added to annual totals. Aggregate categories are excluded from individual-crop rankings.

The national historical data support long-run descriptive comparisons. Recent state data are incomplete and do not support robust long-run state trends. The district dataset supports only a 2024–25 cross-section. The progressive Rabi area-sown report is not a yield dataset.

## Intended Output

The project produces comparable, provenance-aware crop summaries, transparent risk screening, and baseline-tested forecasts where data volume permits. It does not estimate causal effects of weather, policy, irrigation, or inputs because those explanatory variables are not included in the supplied yield table.
