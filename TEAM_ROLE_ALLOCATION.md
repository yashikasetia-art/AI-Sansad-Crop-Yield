# Team Role Allocation

For a four-person team; combine roles if only three speakers are available.

| Role | Ownership | Debate responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Product and data lead | Problem framing, source audit, annual/season definitions | Opening, data provenance, missingness and scope answers |
| 2. Statistics lead | CV, detrending, downside risk, classes and rankings | Method defence, formulas, threshold limitations |
| 3. ML and backend lead | Chronological models, baseline, anomaly detection, API/database | Leakage, validation, model usefulness, system architecture |
| 4. Demo and policy lead | Dashboard, exports, deployment, impact framing | Demonstration, practical resolution, conclusion and rebuttals |

## Speaking Handoff

1. Speaker 1 frames the policy question and states that yield is distinct from area and production.
2. Speaker 2 explains relative variability, detrending, and downside metrics.
3. Speaker 3 demonstrates model evaluation and the baseline comparison.
4. Speaker 4 explains responsible deployment, limitations, and a collaborative resolution.

All speakers should use the same definition of annual `Total`, the same observation period, and the same documented model split.
